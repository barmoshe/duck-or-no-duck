# Everything is Crab reference pack

Reference material for Duck or no duck, pulled 2026-09-26 from the community wiki
(https://everythingiscrab.wiki.gg, content CC BY-SA; game art belongs to the game's
developers). **Reference only:** nothing in this folder ships in `public/` or the game.
Our art is drawn by hand in `game/scripts/art/`.

The wiki's pages and API sit behind a bot check: read them through a real browser. The
`/images/*.png` files download fine with curl and a full Chrome user-agent.

| Folder | What | Count |
|---|---|---|
| `evolutions/` | evolution icons (attack, dash, ultimate, passive, branching) | 107 |
| `specialisations/` | specialisation icons | 38 |
| `genetics/` | genetic icons | 24 |
| `food/` | every food sprite by type and rarity, the 4-stage "being eaten" strips, health food, boss fruit, mushrooms | 28 |
| `ui/` | affinity emblems, slot-type marks, mutagen, POI, boss, alpha icons | 15 |

- [evolutions.md](evolutions.md): every evolution and specialisation with type,
  affinities, levels, lowest rarity, requires/unlocks/bans, and level-1 vs max numbers.
- [mechanics.md](mechanics.md): food and eating, evolution rules (rarity, levels,
  upgrade, mutagen), affinities, and genetics.
